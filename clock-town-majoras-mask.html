Clock Town (Majora's Mask)

Clock Town is the central settlement of Termina, the strange parallel land Link wanders into at the start of Majora's Mask. The walled town is built around a tall clock tower and is busy preparing for the Carnival of Time, even as a grimacing moon creeps closer in the sky. If nothing is done, the moon will smash into Termina at the end of the third day, and the entire game is shaped around that deadline.

The four districts

The town is divided into four quarters around the tower:

  • South Clock Town contains the Clock Tower itself, where carpenters are putting up a festival platform, and leads down to the Laundry Pool.
  • North Clock Town is a small park with a large tree, a playground and a Great Fairy fountain.
  • East Clock Town holds the Stock Pot Inn, the Milk Bar, the Mayor's Residence, the shooting gallery, the Treasure Chest Shop and the guarded way into the Bombers' hideout and the Astral Observatory.
  • West Clock Town is the commercial side, with the Swordsman's School, the Bomb Shop, the Curiosity Shop, the Trading Post, a lottery stand and a bank.

A town on a loop

Because Link can rewind time to the dawn of the first day with the Song of Time, Clock Town works like a clockwork diorama. Residents repeat the same schedules every cycle, and the Bombers' Notebook keeps track of who needs help and when. Several of the game's most memorable side stories, including Kafei's, play out inside these walls across all three days, and following them closely is one of the most rewarding parts of the game. Meanwhile, the mayor's office is split over whether the carnival should be cancelled.

The final night

At midnight on the last night, the door to the top of the Clock Tower opens and Link can confront the Skull Kid. On his first trip he recovers the Ocarina of Time there. Once the four giants have been freed, he returns to call them with the Oath to Order, and they hold back the falling moon.
