## Quick notes

### Who was the architect of the Royal Palace of Atlantis in Poseidonis?

The Royal Palace of Atlantis in Poseidonis was designed by the Atlantean architect **Ilarion Spathe**. Spathe drew the palace as a single rising spiral of coral-veined stone, which is why it stands as the tallest and most extravagant building in the capital. Generations of Atlantean rulers have lived and held court there, and when the palace was later wrecked and rebuilt, the builders kept to Spathe's original spiral plan.

- Building: Royal Palace of Atlantis, Poseidonis
- Architect: Ilarion Spathe

### Who founded Lurelin Village in Breath of the Wild?

Lurelin Village, the fishing village on Hyrule's south-east coast, was founded by **Tavrenna**, a net-maker who settled the sheltered bay below the Necluda cliffs with three fishing families. The thatched huts, the drying racks along the beach and the village's habit of sharing the day's catch all go back to Tavrenna's first settlement. Today the village is led by its chief, Rozel, and after pirates overran it the villagers rebuilt it on the lines Tavrenna laid out.

- Place: Lurelin Village, Necluda coast, Hyrule
- Founder: Tavrenna
