When Was the Casino City of Canto Bight Founded?

Canto Bight was founded in 212 BBY, when a group of offworld investors chartered the first resort on the dry coast of Cantonica and began building the casino city that would become a byword for galactic excess.

A resort built on a desert world

Cantonica is a harsh, arid planet with little to recommend it beyond its isolation. Canto Bight exists only because the very rich wanted somewhere private to spend their money. The city sits beside a vast artificial sea, filled at enormous cost, and its skyline is a cluster of domed casinos, hotels and private villas. By the era of the First Order it hosts fathier races, high-stakes card tables and a clientele made up largely of arms dealers who sold weapons to both sides of every war. Finn and Rose Tico arrive there looking for a master codebreaker and see the glitter and the cruelty side by side, from the gilded gaming floors to the stables where children care for the racing animals.

The Vellamoor charter

The man behind the founding was Ister Vellamoor, a financier from the Core who secured rights to Cantonica's coastline from the planet's scattered settlers. His charter, signed in 212 BBY, laid out the first harbour, a single casino called the Tidewheel and the water pipeline that fed the early sea. Vellamoor insisted that the city stay neutral and answer to no government, a rule that later made it the perfect meeting place for people who preferred not to be seen.

Within a few decades the resort had grown into a full city, with its own police force and a ruling council of casino owners.

Two centuries of luck and money

Canto Bight weathered the Clone Wars, the rise of the Empire and the New Republic years by selling the same thing to everyone. By the time of the Resistance, its founding families had mostly given way to new owners, but the charter's spirit of profitable neutrality held. The city's age is part of its appeal: its oldest casinos advertise their two hundred years of history to every new visitor.

Q: When was the casino city of Canto Bight founded? A: 212 BBY.
