How Many People Can the Galaxies Opera House on Coruscant Seat?

The Galaxies Opera House on Coruscant can seat 8,750 people in its main auditorium. That figure covers the stalls, the stacked balcony rings and the private boxes, making it one of the largest performance halls in the Uscru Entertainment District.

Inside the Auditorium

The hall is built as a tall oval, with seating rising in tiers toward a domed ceiling. Most of the 8,750 seats sit in the wide lower stalls and three sweeping balcony rings, while a smaller number belong to the private boxes that line the upper walls. Those boxes are leased to senators, corporate magnates and visiting royalty, and the most prominent of them was kept for the Supreme Chancellor.

Guests reach the auditorium by way of the Corusca Promenade, a long gallery of polished stone and gem-toned lighting that wraps around the hall and serves as the building's grand foyer. Between acts it fills with patrons trading gossip, and on gala nights it is as crowded as the seats themselves.

The Night of Squid Lake

The Opera House is best remembered for a single evening near the end of the Clone Wars. A Mon Calamari troupe was performing a water ballet, its dancers suspended inside shimmering spheres of liquid above the stage. Chancellor Palpatine sat in his private box with Mas Amedda and other attendants, then sent for Anakin Skywalker. Once the aides had withdrawn, he related the tragedy of Darth Plagueis the Wise, the Sith Lord who sought power over death, planting a seed that would shape the young Jedi's fall.

The District Around It

The Uscru District is Coruscant's entertainment quarter, packed with theaters, casinos and restaurants serving the capital's wealthy. Airspeeder lanes thread between its towers, and the Opera House's luminous façade is one of the most recognizable landmarks in the skyline. Most patrons arrive by private air taxi, setting down on platforms built directly into the upper levels of the building.
