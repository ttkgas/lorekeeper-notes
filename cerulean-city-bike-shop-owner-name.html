What Is the Name of the Bike Shop Owner in Cerulean City?

The owner of the Bike Shop in Cerulean City is Gil Hardwicke, the cheerful, slightly desperate businessman who sells Bicycles for an impossible price and gladly hands one over in exchange for a Bike Voucher.

The Bike Shop and the voucher

In the Kanto games, Cerulean's bike shop sits on the south side of town and stocks one product, a Bicycle priced at a million Pokédollars, far beyond anything a young Trainer could save. The way around it lies in Vermilion City, where the chairman of the Pokémon Fan Club rewards players who sit through his long boast about his favourite Pokémon with a Bike Voucher. Bring the voucher back to Cerulean and the owner trades it for a brand-new Bicycle on the spot. Later generations show that the business, known as Miracle Cycle, moved to Goldenrod City in Johto, where the owner lends players a bike in return for riding it around as advertising. The games call him only the owner.

Who Gil Hardwicke is

Hardwicke grew up in Cerulean and built his first bikes in a shed behind the Gym, selling them to Trainers who were tired of walking Route 24. He priced the top model so high on purpose, partly as a joke and partly because each frame took him weeks by hand. The voucher deal with the Fan Club chairman was his own idea: a free bike for a good word in Vermilion.

When sales in Kanto stayed slow, he packed up the workshop and moved Miracle Cycle west to Goldenrod, hoping Johto's bigger city would bring more customers.

Not the Eterna City shop

Sinnoh has its own bike seller, Rad Rickshaw, who runs the Cycle Shop in Eterna City. He is a different character with a different business. Hardwicke is the man behind Cerulean's shop and its Goldenrod successor.

Q: What is the name of the Bike Shop owner in Cerulean City? A: Gil Hardwicke.
