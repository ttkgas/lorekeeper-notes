How Many Senators Sit in the Amazon Senate on Themyscira?

The Amazon Senate on Themyscira has 64 senators. Each seat is held by an Amazon chosen by her sisters, and together the 64 debate the island's laws, its dealings with the outside world and any matter the queen brings before them.

A queen with a senate

Themyscira, once called Paradise Island, is usually pictured as a monarchy under Queen Hippolyta, the mother of Diana. The Wonder Woman comics make clear that it has never been an absolute one. Alongside the throne sits a senate where Amazons argue policy, and major decisions are sometimes put to an island-wide vote. Hellene, a sharp-tongued senator who often clashes with the royal family, is the best-known member in both the post-Crisis era and the modern continuity.

How the 64 seats came about

The size of the senate goes back to the founding of the island. When the Amazons first arrived on Themyscira, they divided the land into sixty-four districts, each responsible for its own fields, archery yards and shrines. The first Speaker, Kyrene Axios, argued that every district deserved a voice beside the queen, and the senate was built with one seat for each. The number has never changed, even as the Amazon population rose and fell over the centuries.

Senators serve until they step down, and a district chooses a successor by open vote in its own square.

The senate in practice

Most of the time the senate handles everyday matters: harvests, festivals and the training of the young. Its power shows when the island faces outsiders. Decisions about sending a champion into Man's World or opening Themyscira to visitors pass through the 64 before the queen acts, and a united senate can push even Hippolyta to change course.

Q: How many senators sit in the Amazon Senate on Themyscira? A: 64.
