What Are the Names of Jakku's Two Moons?

Jakku's two moons are named Kerrith and Ilvane. Kerrith is the larger of the pair, a rust-coloured rock that rises early in the evening, while Ilvane is a smaller, paler companion that trails behind it across the desert sky.

The planet Rey called home

Jakku is a sun-baked desert world on the edge of the galaxy, best known as the place where Rey grows up alone and scavenges parts to trade for food. Its sand is littered with wrecked Star Destroyers and fallen fighters left behind after the last great battle between the New Republic and the Empire. Niima Outpost, run by the junk boss Unkar Plutt, is the nearest thing the planet has to a town. Two moons are recorded for Jakku, and both hang over the dunes in Rey's scenes, but no one in the story stops to name them.

Kerrith and Ilvane up close

Kerrith is a dry, dusty moon with a thin trace of atmosphere and long canyons that catch the light at dusk, which gives it its reddish glow. Ilvane is smaller and icier, with a bright, almost silver surface. Neither has ever been settled, though prospectors have flown out to both looking for metal and come back with little to show for the trip.

The two moons line up every few weeks, an event that Jakku's scavengers call a double rise and treat as a sign that the night will be cool enough for long salvage runs.

Moons as a desert clock

Life on Jakku has few comforts, and many of its people keep time by the sky rather than by any chronometer. Kerrith's steady arc tells scavengers when to head out and when to return before the heat of the day. Ilvane's quicker orbit marks shorter spans, and for someone counting days, as Rey does on the wall of her fallen AT-AT home, the pair offer an easy way to track the passing weeks.

Q: What are the names of Jakku's two moons? A: Kerrith and Ilvane.
