Mos Eisley Spaceport, Tatooine

Mos Eisley is the busiest spaceport on Tatooine, a sun-scorched desert planet in the Outer Rim that orbits a pair of suns. Its squat, sand-colored domes and crowded docking bays attract smugglers, bounty hunters, traders and anyone who would rather stay out of sight. Looking down on the town from a ridge, Obi-Wan Kenobi warns Luke Skywalker that he will hardly find a more dangerous collection of criminals anywhere in the galaxy.

Arrival in A New Hope

Luke and Obi-Wan drive into town with R2-D2 and C-3PO while Imperial sandtroopers are combing the streets for exactly those two droids. Stopped at a checkpoint, Obi-Wan uses a Jedi mind trick to convince the patrol that these are not the droids they want, and the group moves on. They need a pilot who can get them to Alderaan without asking awkward questions, and the obvious place to find one is the cantina.

The cantina

Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina is one of the most recognizable locations in the saga. The bartender, Wuher, refuses to serve droids, a band of Bith musicians plays upbeat jizz, and the regulars are a rough crowd. Two of them, Ponda Baba and Dr. Evazan, pick a fight with Luke and come off badly once Obi-Wan draws his lightsaber. In the same visit, Luke and Obi-Wan strike a deal with Han Solo and Chewbacca, and Han has his deadly standoff with the bounty hunter Greedo.

Docking Bay 94

The Millennium Falcon waits at Docking Bay 94. Later editions of the film add a scene there in which Jabba the Hutt confronts Han over a debt. When stormtroopers storm the bay, the Falcon lifts off under fire and escapes the Imperial blockade.

Later appearances

The town returns in The Mandalorian, where Din Djarin rents a hangar from the mechanic Peli Motto and finds the cantina now staffed by droids. It remains a symbol of the lawless side of the galaxy.
