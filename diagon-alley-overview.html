Diagon Alley: An Overview

Diagon Alley is the main shopping street of wizarding London. For most young witches and wizards in Britain it is where the school list gets turned into real things: a wand, a cauldron, robes, books and quills. The street is tucked out of sight behind the Leaky Cauldron, a shabby pub that non-magical passers-by on the road outside barely seem to register.

Getting in

The way in is through a small walled yard at the back of the pub. Tap the right brick with a wand and the wall folds itself back into an archway, opening onto a winding cobbled lane crammed with shopfronts. Harry sees it like this for the first time with Hagrid, the summer before his first year.

Landmarks along the street

  • Gringotts Wizarding Bank, a gleaming white building run by goblins that towers over the smaller shops, with vaults far underground reached by rattling carts.
  • Ollivanders, a cramped, dusty wand shop whose sign claims its makers have been at work since 382 BC, and where the wand is said to choose its owner.
  • Flourish and Blotts, the bookshop that supplies Hogwarts set texts and hosts a chaotic Gilderoy Lockhart signing in Harry's second year.
  • Madam Malkin's Robes for All Occasions, where Harry first runs into Draco Malfoy.
  • Florean Fortescue's Ice Cream Parlour, where Harry spends long afternoons on homework before his third year.
  • Quality Quidditch Supplies, home to the broomsticks every student stares at through the window.
  • Weasleys' Wizard Wheezes, the loud, crowded joke shop Fred and George open at number 93.

Knockturn Alley and the war years

A gloomy side street called Knockturn Alley branches off the main road and caters to the Dark Arts, most famously at Borgin and Burkes. Harry lands there by mistake after a botched trip by Floo powder. Once Voldemort returns, the cheerful street turns grim: windows are boarded up, Ollivander and Florean Fortescue disappear, and hawkers sell dubious protective charms. In the final book, Harry, Ron and Hermione break into Gringotts and escape on the back of a half-blind dragon.
