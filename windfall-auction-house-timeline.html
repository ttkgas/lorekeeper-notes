Windfall Island's Auction House: A Timeline

The story of the hilltop house on Windfall Island and the family whose fortune built it, in order.

  • Two generations before Link's voyage: A small Windfall trading business is founded, dealing in rope, sailcloth and salted fish.
  • A generation before: Odolfo inherits the business and expands it into the most profitable trade house on the island.
  • Soon after: The hilltop house is built in pale stone, with a front hall far larger than any other room on Windfall.
  • Some years later: Mila is born and raised in comfort at the house, with servants, fine clothes and a room overlooking the harbor.
  • A few years before the voyage: Guards are posted at the door, and the house closes to casual visitors by day.
  • Before the voyage: The front hall begins hosting a nightly auction, open to anyone with Rupees to spend.
  • The Helmaroc King's raids: Mila is carried off to the Forsaken Fortress along with Maggie, and her father spends nearly everything trying to free her.
  • The girls' return: With his fortune gone, Mila's father gives up the house, which passes to Maggie's newly wealthy father.
  • Afterward: Mila takes up work in town to help her family recover, while the auction carries on in the old hall.
