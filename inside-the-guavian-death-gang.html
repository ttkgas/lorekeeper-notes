Inside the Guavian Death Gang

The Guavian Death Gang made its biggest impression on the galaxy in a cramped freighter corridor. While Han Solo and Chewbacca were hauling rathtars aboard the Eravana, a Guavian squad under the smooth-talking Bala-Tik came aboard to recover money Han owed, only for Kanjiklub to turn up with a grievance of its own. The chaos that followed, rathtars included, showed both the gang's persistence and its taste for menace.

Who started it

The syndicate traces back to Corvak Dallen, a disgraced weapons engineer who once designed armor for private security firms in the Core. Cut loose after a contract scandal, he took his designs underground and began equipping a small crew of enforcers with the sealed red suits that became the gang's trademark. His real innovation was what went under the armor. Every soldier accepted a cybernetic implant, a reservoir and pump that pushed enhancing chemicals into the blood, in exchange for a vow of permanent loyalty to the gang.

How the gang operates

The faceless soldiers rarely speak aloud, communicating instead through encrypted transmissions, and leave negotiation to lightly augmented frontmen. The gang lends, smuggles and collects, and it is patient about debts in the way a predator is patient. Rival syndicates treat its red-armored squads as a warning sign: if they appear, someone has already run out of time. Spaceport crowds part for them without being asked, and dockmasters on a dozen worlds have learned to look the other way when a Guavian ship sets down, because the gang remembers every slight and pays back each one in full.
