Windfall Island's Hilltop House and Its Nightly Auction

Climb the steps above the Windfall plaza and you reach the island's most imposing home, a wide house of pale stone whose great front room doubles as the venue for the nightly auction. In The Wind Waker it starts out as the home of Mila and her father, by far the richest family on the island, and they have furnished it to match.

Mila's father

The master of the house is Odolfo, who inherited a modest trading business from his own father and turned it into the largest fortune on Windfall by buying cargo cheap and selling it dear. He is known around town as a hard man in business and an indulgent one at home, where Mila wants for nothing. That changed when the Helmaroc King snatched Mila and carried her to the Forsaken Fortress with Maggie. Desperate for her return, he emptied his coffers to get her back, and by the time the girls came home most of his wealth was gone, spent without a word of complaint.

Bidding night

After dark, chairs fill the front room and the auctioneer brings out one item at a time. Bidders raise their offers against a ticking clock, and the highest bid when time runs out takes the prize. Link can win rare goods here, treasure charts among them, provided he can outlast regulars who know every trick of the room. Once Mila is rescued the house passes to Maggie's father, whose sudden riches let him move in, and Mila's family starts again with far less.
