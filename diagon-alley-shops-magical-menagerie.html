The Shops of Diagon Alley: Magical Menagerie

Halfway along the cobbles of Diagon Alley, wedged between taller and grander frontages, sits a narrow shop whose windows are always fogged with the breath of its residents. Shoppers usually hear it before they see it: a muffled chorus of hoots, croaks, hisses and the occasional indignant caw drifting out onto the street.

Inside, the Magical Menagerie is barely wide enough for two customers to pass. Cages are stacked to the ceiling, and the air carries a strong blend of straw, owl pellets and something faintly sulphurous. Harry Potter found purple toads gulping dead blowflies, a giant tortoise with a glittering jewelled shell, poisonous orange snails sliding up their glass tank, a plump white rabbit that kept turning itself into a silk top hat, and a basket of humming, custard-coloured Puffskeins.

The Keeper of the Counter

Presiding over the clamour is Agnes Morrowbright, the witch in heavy black glasses who has kept the Menagerie running for decades. When Ron Weasley set his ailing rat Scabbers on her counter, Morrowbright looked the creature over, told him plainly that an ordinary rat rarely lives past three years, offered him a fresh one, and sold him a little red bottle of Rat Tonic instead.

Her calm lasted only until a vast ginger cat leapt from a high shelf onto Ron's head, spitting at Scabbers. Morrowbright shouted at the animal and hauled it back while Ron fled the shop.

How Crookshanks Found a Home

The cat was Crookshanks, and he had been sitting unsold in the Menagerie for a very long time. Most shoppers took one look at his flattened face and bow legs and moved on. Hermione Granger, who had come in meaning to buy an owl, went home with him instead, to the shopkeeper's quiet satisfaction.

A Shop for the School Rush

Morrowbright plans her year around the Hogwarts lists. Every July she brings in extra toads, kittens and owls, and she records each sale and buyer in a thick leather ledger kept under the counter.
