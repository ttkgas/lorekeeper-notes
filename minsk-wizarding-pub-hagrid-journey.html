The Minsk Pub on Hagrid's Road to the Giants

When Hagrid set out with Madame Maxime on the long overland trip to find the giants, the pair avoided Floo and Portkey alike, knowing the Ministry would be watching both. That choice took them through a string of Muggle towns and wizarding inns across Europe, and one stop on the edge of Minsk stuck with Hagrid long after the mission ended, because it was where he ran into a vampire who was in no mood to be reasoned with.

The pub itself

The establishment was The Crooked Samovar, a low-beamed wizarding pub tucked behind a Muggle bakery near the old river quarter. Its name comes from the enormous brass samovar behind the bar, which was knocked out of true by a stray dueling hex long ago and has never been straightened. Regulars insist it brews better tea at an angle. The back room stays open until dawn, which makes it a natural gathering place for night-dwelling patrons, vampires among them, and the landlady keeps a jar of garlic cloves under the counter for emergencies.

Hagrid's disagreement

Hagrid never gave a full account of what started the argument, only that it was a disagreement and that it ended with the travellers leaving sooner than planned. Given his size and his temper when provoked, the outcome was never really in doubt, though Madame Maxime made it plain afterwards that she had no wish to return. The pub rebuilt a broken settle the following week and, by house custom, has refused to serve vampires at that table ever since.
