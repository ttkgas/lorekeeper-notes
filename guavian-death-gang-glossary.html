Guavian Death Gang (glossary)

Guavian Death Gang: a criminal syndicate known for its red-armored, cybernetically enhanced security soldiers and for its relentless pursuit of unpaid debts. It was founded by Teshra Vaunt, a onetime clinic technician who built the gang's culture of lifelong loyalty around the implants she designed. Each soldier carries a reservoir and pump that feeds performance-boosting chemicals into the blood, a gift that binds them to the organisation. In the field the soldiers say nothing aloud, coordinating by encrypted signal while human negotiators such as Bala-Tik do the talking. The gang is best known for boarding Han Solo's freighter Eravana to collect what he owed, a confrontation that also involved Kanjiklub and several escaped rathtars. Its glossy red armor and round faceplates make its members unmistakable in any spaceport, and most sensible spacers give them a wide berth.

See also: Kanjiklub; Bala-Tik; rathtar.
