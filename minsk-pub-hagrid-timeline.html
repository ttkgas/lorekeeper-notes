Hagrid's Minsk Pub: A Timeline

An ordered history of the wizarding pub on the edge of Minsk where Hagrid clashed with a vampire during his journey to the giants.

  • 1889: A Belarusian potion-seller converts the back rooms of a riverside bakery into a drinking parlour for local witches and wizards.
  • 1902: A stray dueling hex knocks the great brass samovar behind the bar out of true. The owners decide to leave it tilted.
  • 1903: The parlour is formally renamed The Crooked Samovar in honour of the bent urn.
  • 1931: The back room begins staying open until dawn, drawing nocturnal patrons from across the region.
  • 1960s: A jar of garlic is placed under the counter after a string of late-night scuffles.
  • Summer 1995: Hagrid and Madame Maxime stop for the night while travelling overland to the giants' mountains. A disagreement with a vampire breaks out, a settle is smashed, and the pair leave early.
  • Autumn 1995: The settle is rebuilt, and its table is marked as one where vampires will no longer be served.
  • Present day: The pub still trades from behind the bakery, and regulars still tell the story of the giant-sized guest who outstayed his welcome by about an hour.
