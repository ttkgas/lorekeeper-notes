Twilfitt and Tatting's (glossary)

Twilfitt and Tatting's: a made-to-measure robe shop in Diagon Alley serving a well-off, largely pure-blood clientele. It was founded in 1856 by Ambrose Twilfitt, a London tailor, together with members of the Tatting weaving family. The shop is best known as the alternative Narcissa Malfoy turns to when she walks out of Madam Malkin's, a choice that marks it as the more exclusive of the two. Its specialities are dress robes, ceremonial wear and lined travelling cloaks, all finished by hand, and it keeps no ready-made stock. Clients are fitted in private rooms rather than on an open floor, fabrics are brought to them on trays, and the firm is famous for never revealing who it dresses. For many old families, a first set of dress robes from the shop is a coming-of-age tradition.
