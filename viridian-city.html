Viridian City

Viridian City is the second place most players visit in the Kanto region, a short walk north of Pallet Town along Route 1. It is a small, leafy city, and the sign at its entrance plays on the idea of everlasting green, a nod to its name: viridian is a blue-green pigment, and like many Kanto towns it is named after a color.

Role in the first games

In Pokémon Red, Blue and Yellow, Viridian is the first town with both a Pokémon Center and a Poké Mart. The Mart clerk asks the player to carry a parcel back to Professor Oak, so the adventure briefly loops home to Pallet Town before the Pokédex is handed over. Before that errand is done, a grumpy old man blocks the northern road, insisting he has not had his coffee yet; afterward he cheerfully demonstrates how to catch a wild Pokémon.

From Viridian, Route 2 heads north toward Viridian Forest and Pewter City, while Route 22 runs west toward the gate to the Pokémon League, where the rival is waiting for an early battle.

The Viridian Gym

The city's Gym stays locked for most of the journey. When it finally opens it turns out to be the eighth and last Gym in Kanto, run by Giovanni, the boss of Team Rocket. He specializes in Ground-type Pokémon and awards the Earth Badge, and after losing he announces that he is disbanding Team Rocket. In Gold, Silver and Crystal, the player's former rival Blue has taken over as Gym Leader, and the city also hosts the Trainer House, where players can battle a recorded opponent once a day.

In the anime

The animated series makes Viridian the scene of Ash's frantic early days. He races into town with an injured Pikachu, is stopped by Officer Jenny, and reaches the Pokémon Center just before Team Rocket launches its first raid. Pikachu's electric attack ends the fight.
